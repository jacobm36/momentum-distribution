# Star Wars Trilogy Arcade

Jump into the original trilogy. Stay on target.

## Bring your game

Bring your compatible swtrilgy.zip. Keep it zipped in its own folder. Game files are not included.

## Install with Momentum

**Add game > Star Wars Trilogy Arcade > Choose folder > Review > Install.**

Momentum prepares required software after approval, copies your game files, and leaves the originals untouched.

## Controls

Wait for the Darth Vader attract sequence. Press 3 for a credit and 1 to start. Mouse to aim, left-click or A to fire, right-click or S for the event button, D for trigger 2. Escape exits.

## Compatibility

Ubuntu 24.04 x86_64, X11, pinned Supermodel 0.3a-20260928-git-8b4de23, fullscreen at 992x768. The guide confirms its historical setup; this isolated package profile still needs your own gameplay confirmation. Bonus-stage mechanics are not separately certified.

Your installation remains **setup required** until you confirm gameplay.

## Where it goes

Game files default to `~/Games/Star Wars Trilogy Arcade`.
Settings and saves stay separate in `~/.local/share/momentum-core/state/star-wars-trilogy-arcade`.
The launcher is `~/.local/bin/star-wars-trilogy-arcade`; support and backups remain
under `~/.local/share/momentum-core/`.
