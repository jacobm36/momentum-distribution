# Brutal Doom 64

Back to the nightmare. Doom 64's maps, monsters and atmosphere meet a louder,
meaner arsenal, with the announcer and music ready to go.

## Bring your Doom II

Choose the folder containing your **DOOM2.WAD**. The complete four-file mod is
included; no separate mod download or file ordering is needed.

## Install with Momentum

**Add game > Brutal Doom 64 > Choose folder > Review > Install.**

Momentum reuses compatible UZDoom without upgrading it. If missing, separately
approve preparation for your account, then review the game-file plan.
Your original IWAD stays untouched.

## Rip, tear, save

Check Options/Controls for movement, mouse aim and firing. Existing UZDoom
settings are preserved; no resolution, fullscreen or input preferences are
forced. Confirm music and the announcer, then save, exit normally and test loading
that save after relaunch. Older mod-version saves may not be compatible.

## Compatibility

Ubuntu 24.04 x86_64 and the exact approved UZDoom 5.0.1 Flatpak build.
No GZDoom or Zandronum fallback. Installer testing is separate from gameplay:
your library entry stays **setup required** until you confirm controls, display,
sound and save/relaunch. The upstream notes recommend disabling Enhanced Night
Vision when changing engine setups; Momentum does not change it for you.

## Keep it yours

Default destination: `~/Games/Brutal Doom 64`. The IWAD sits beside
`mods/BrutalDoom64`, loaded as gameplay, maps, announcer, then music.
Access is granted read-only for each launch, not through persistent overrides.
UZDoom settings and saves remain under `~/.var/app/org.zdoom.UZDoom/`.

Verify is read-only. Repair restores missing files from their original bytes.
Uninstall removes only unchanged owned mod/integration files, keeping your IWAD,
state, modified files, artwork and shared engine. Retained receipts allow
reinstall without replacing saves.

The launcher is `~/.local/bin/brutal-doom-64`. Lifecycle support and backups
remain under `~/.local/share/momentum-core/`. See the player guide for play notes.
