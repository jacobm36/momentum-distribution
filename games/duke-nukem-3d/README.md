# Duke Nukem 3D

Alien invaders, outrageous weapons, and attitude to spare. Blast through Los
Angeles streets, space stations, and secret-filled strongholds as Earth's
wisecracking one-man army. Shrink enemies, set pipe-bomb traps, and strap on a
jetpack to reach hidden routes. Interactive scenery, inventive weapons, and
explosive firefights make exploration as entertaining as clearing the next room.

## Bring your Duke

Choose the folder containing **DUKE3D.GRP** from your registered original or
Atomic Edition copy. Optional **DUKE.RTS** and root settings/saves travel with it.
Shareware is not supported; World Tour and expansions need separate checks.

## Install with Momentum

**Add game > Duke Nukem 3D > Choose folder > Review > Install.**

Momentum reuses your compatible native EDuke32 without changing it. When absent,
the runner publishes the exact bundled working EDuke32 build after separate
approval. No engine download or compilation is required. Its matching pinned
source archive, build information and upstream notices are included.
The engine still needs its Ubuntu host libraries; Momentum does not elevate or
install system packages. A different existing engine is preserved and remains
gated, not silently replaced.

Default destination: `~/Games/duke-nukem-3d`. Existing receipt-recorded folders
keep their names. Your original folder, engine and shared settings stay untouched.
This package requires Momentum launcher preview.2 or newer with bundled EDuke32
support. The initial public preview.1 does not include it; update the launcher
rather than bypassing a blocked older launcher.

## Duke it out

Check the game's Controls menu for movement, mouse aim and firing. Use Polymost,
Voxels On, High Resolution Textures On and Music On. Absent local settings receive
starting presets; existing preferences are preserved, not forced.

Fullscreen is requested without forcing a resolution or rewriting settings.
Use the launch wrapper's `-window` argument for a windowed test. Confirm all
three packs in a campaign level, then save, exit normally and test loading that
save after relaunch. Atomic Edition's fourth-episode voxel coverage is not
promised.

## Keep it yours

Settings and saves live in this isolated game folder. Verify is read-only;
repair restores missing owned files and refuses edited managed files.
Uninstall keeps your base files, settings, saves, artwork and shared engine.
Retained-state reinstall brings the enhancements back without replacing saves.

Ubuntu 24.04 x86_64, exact approved native EDuke32 bytes. No alternative engine
fallback. Installer verification is separate from gameplay. The owner reported
that the candidate looks amazing and approved publication on October 25, 2026;
full campaign and individual control/audio/save-relaunch checks are still not
claimed.
