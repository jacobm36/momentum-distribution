=====
ABOUT
=====

This pack is meant for those who want to play Duke Nukem 3D with the Highres Pack (HRP) 
and also like to have high quality music instead of listening to the old MIDI soundtrack.
It also works for anyone who just wants to have a music replacement for the game without
having any HRP installed.

The music was recorded by MusicallyInspired with a Roland SC-55 synthesizer.



=====
USAGE
=====

Place this zipfile (duke3d_music-sc55.zip) into your "autoload" dir of your EDuke32
installation. It does not matter whether you have any HRP installed or not, the music
pack will work.
