# The House of the Dead 2

The dead don't wait. Neither should your next arcade run.

## Bring your game

Your Dreamcast USA CUE and all matching BIN tracks, together in one folder.
Game files are not included.

## Get going

**Momentum > Add game > The House of the Dead 2 > Choose folder > Review > Install.**

Momentum prepares the required software after approval and leaves your original
game files untouched.

## Controls

Mouse to aim, left-click to fire, Enter to start. Confirm off-screen reload
and your preferred controls in gameplay.

## Compatibility

Installer 0.1.3 targets Ubuntu 24.04 x86_64 with the exact supported Flycast v2.7
build. The isolated HOD2 setup has gameplay evidence; confirm your own installation
and saved progress too.

[Download installer 0.1.3](https://github.com/jacobm36/momentum-distribution/releases/tag/the-house-of-the-dead-2-0.1.3)
