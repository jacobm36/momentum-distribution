GENERAL INFORMATION:
My first Quake map. I only decided to make a map less than two weeks before the deadline so my time was pretty limited to come up with something while also learning Trenchbroom, hence the map's length. I would have liked to make something a bit bigger with a lot more visual polish, but getting something complete at all felt like a victory. Working with the QBJ3 mod has been a ton of fun, and finally working with brushes after years of Doom mapping is very freeing :D
