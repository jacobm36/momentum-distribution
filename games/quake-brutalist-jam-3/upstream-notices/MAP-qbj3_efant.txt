Title                   : You've Been Tagged and Now You're It
Filename                : qbj3_efant.bsp
Author                  : efant

Description             :
                        Your interdimensional travel got intercepted and now you find yourself on some sort of playground.
                        Escape the forces responsible for your abduction. Locate both Silver and Gold Rune Keys, and slip through another gateway!

                        "You've Been Tagged and Now You're It" is a map in a style of Brutalist playground with just two keys, obstacles and structures for (hopefully!) joyful movement.
                        Made for Quake Brutalist Jam 3.
