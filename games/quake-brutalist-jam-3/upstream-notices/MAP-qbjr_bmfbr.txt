Entry for the Quake Brutalist Jam 3.

Basically a mild remix of my original QBJ1 entry (Fourth Vertex of Trapezoid). Updated textures with
Makkon's latest concrete pack, various gameplay balancing changes to accomodate the QBJ3 roster
and arsenal, miscellaneous geometry and texturing fixes, a bit of switchable shadow abuse, added
coop spawn points, and did a great deal of optimization that cut down the BSP+LIT to less than 1/4
of the original size.

It was now compiled with full vis (YES!), but light was compiled only with -extra instead of
-extra4 (NO!) otherwise it'd take more than a full day to run on my 10yo CPU. Compiled with
ericw-tools 2.0 alpha10 - it's a lot more feature-packed but unfortunately way slower as well.

Tested on Ironwail, but should run fine on any QS fork. Protocol 999 is recommended but that should
already be enforced by the QBJ3 devkit.

Like the original, it has no music track set. Enjoy all the ambiences around the map.

Skybox was modified from the original for Ironwail skywind support. If you're on another port you
won't see the glorious moving clouds.
