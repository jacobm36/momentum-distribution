Description:
	Medium-small airborne/platforming map for Quake Brutalist Jam 3 (2025).
	Remake of map (qbj2_nickster) from Quake Brutalist Jam 2 (2023).
	Go way up high, then deep down low.
Requires Mod?
	Yes - QBJ3
Requires Source Port?
	Yes - tested with
		IronWail (https://github.com/andrei-drexler/ironwail)

---------------------------------------
YOU SHOULD HAVE INSTALLED THIS WITH THE JAM, BUT JUST IN CASE

Installation:
	Unzip the archive into your quake/qbj3 folder
To Play:
	Start QBJ3
	Open the console (~)
	Type "skill #" where # is 0 (easy), 1 (normal), 2 (hard), 3 (nightmare) and hit enter
	Type "map qbjr_nickster" and hit enter
	Hold onto your butt
