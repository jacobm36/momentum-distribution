-+-Ennaum Teferet-+-
ABOUT
This is my first Quake map, so do excuse any ugly or unnecessary brushwork (or combat, or lighting, or any weirdness in general).
I tried to make a large enclosed space, so there's no outdoors section.

PLAY INFO
Co-op starts provided. The map isn't balanced for it beyond this.
All difficulties have been balanced for. Ultimately, it's not very hard but not a piece of cake either.
Completion time is around 15-20 minutes, maybe a bit less.
