-About-
Two pretty bad things that came in under Thatcher were Section 28, part of an act of parliament that among other things functionally banned any discussion of LGBT topics in schools. It had impacted a lot of lives including mine. The other was the destruction of widely available social housing, at the peak of which ~30% of the population lived in.

I'm also always a little sad that brutalism has a strong association with authoritarian dystopias when much of its origin was in post WW2 reconstruction and social housing development, so I decided to smash all these things that annoy me together into a blob. I hope you like it, or don't.
