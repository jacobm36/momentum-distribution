Changes from the originaL:
*I beefed up the combat from the original map to spice things up and take advantage of the new weapons and enemies. One of the complaints about the first map was that every fight felt worse after the garage encounter. I addressed this by adding a quad and more enemies to each main fight.

*I made the teleporting ball that kills Shub move faster so that if you miss it, you’re not stuck waiting forever.

*You can now exit the level from either side of the train, and there’s a wider exit trigger along with teleporter textures indicating how to leave. No more getting stuck without knowing what to do!
  
*I replaced the trees with sprites because I thought they looked better.

*Minor visual improvements have been made throughout.

Comments:
I'd like to think that if quake had a sequel in the vein of Doom II that had the demons invading earth this is how it'd look like, hence the multiple doom refrences.
