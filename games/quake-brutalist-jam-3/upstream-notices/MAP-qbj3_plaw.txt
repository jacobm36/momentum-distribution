* Construction *
After the huge success of QBJ2 in 2023 I've sworn to myself that I'd participate in the next installment of the jam. And here we are.
I've finally a good reason to return from my Quake mapping hiatus and fire up Trenchbroom again.
Still can't believe how massive this mod has become and how high of a production value the people behind it have managed to achieve.
The mod's gameplay is much faster and I had to limit myself to a certain size due to time constraints.
Most players will finish this one in less than 20 minutes, but that's totally fine.

* Running qbj3_plaw.bsp *
You'll require the qbj3 mod to play.
Copy qbj3_plaw.bsp and qbj3_plaw.lit into the qbj3/maps folder of your Quake installation.
Also copy the mp3 from the music folder into the qbj3/music folder.
Start Quake and run the qbj3 mod (type 'game qbj3' in the console).
You can either select the map from the start map or run it directly by typing into the cosole:
    map qbj3_plaw
