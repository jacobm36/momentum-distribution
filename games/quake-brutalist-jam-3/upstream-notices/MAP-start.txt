Notes:
	My initial idea for this map was an oddly well kept but empty earthscraper (inverted skyscraper) built into a desolate wasteland, with miles of meat below the thin surface that was overtaking the interior.
after getting over 70 map entries, that idea was no longer viable, so I went for more of a museum/institution setting. Some of the meat is still present, at the start and in a secret. overall the map is meant to be more surreal, cerebral, like a place that exists in your memory rather than an actual place.
The stark desolate colorlessness of the opening contrasting with the vibrant and welcoming interior is, at least for this series, a welcome twist. You can't understate the effectiveness of carpet, rugs, and furniture to make even a bunker feel cozy.
	There is a secret encounter hidden in the map designed by Fairweather.
