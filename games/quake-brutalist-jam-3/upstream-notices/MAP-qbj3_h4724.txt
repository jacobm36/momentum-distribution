Notes:
	I started this map for QBJ2 way back in September 2023. Once the 
deadline passed, I set it aside to work on other things and hoped to maybe
release it standalone or as part of QBJ3. Of course, I didn't know at that
point that QBJ3 would come 2 years later and include a total conversion.
	The initial inspiration for the visuals came from some pictures I
took of the cargo unloading area of a shopping mall. It doesn't look like
the map at all, but it was a starting point for the facade through which 
you exit the starting area, as well as the overwhelming sense of space that
I wanted to capture. I blended various styles and purposes, including
utilitarian industrial spaces, monumental/sculptural shapes, utopian public
spaces, and military bunkers, along with crystalline growths of concrete
that aren't meant to have been built at all. I didn't try to fit a specific
backstory; it's all somewhat dreamlike and nonsensical, just like Quake,
but I did try to execute on some clear ideas.
	I'm happy with the highly vertical layout, and I think there are
some fun gameplay moments, but it's a lot less tightly designed than some
of my other maps. I don't think that's so bad, especially when I wanted the
focus to be more on the visuals, but it does mean that some of the
encounters get messy and less fun depending on how you play them.
