*about/notes*

This is my first map :D I started with a sketch of 3 leafs 120 degrees apart, 
but then I learned that that would be really hard, so i adapted the idea to 3 
sections to the rooms its in now. I like the maps I’ve played where you have a 
center focus point and slowly unlock it, if i had more time/experience I would 
have made more backtracking and puzzles(I had to scrap a lot of idea). After 
finishing the main layout of the sanctum, I realized it might be too short, 
so i was going to make the same temple but red themed, like you go through the center
portal and end up doing it all over again but its the dark version. I ran into 
some issues with this so I had to scrap. Instead I went with more of a Signalis (which i played earlier this year)
vibe for the end room(it was really red), but i had to change colors cause i cant have 
2 skyboxes. growing pains i guess. really inspired by brutalist architecture that only 
uses light to illuminate its interiors. There wasn’t a specific real life counterpart 
or inspiration, I just tried to approximate what was in my head. I went with a sky texture 
that i felt would mimic the feeling of being inside with your eyes adjusted to the light, 
with the outside light being extremely bright. it comes off a bit more surreal but 
thats a happy accident ig lol. I made the short track that repeats, also kinda inspired 
by Signalis with the resonant peaks that come up at a consistent interval. layers of noise
and ambient sound tracks with differnet effect and a melody that comes in to break up the
noise that fills the empty sanctum. I'll call the track, Ternary Sanctum after the map, 
but its just track25 lol. Shout out to Fizz Vol.1 for carrying my life through these troubling times.

Thanks for playing and reading this section. I can't wait to make more maps! 
(hope this one wasn't too bad)
