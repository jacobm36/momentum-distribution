Comments: This map draws heavy inspiration from my favorite sci-fi movies, video games, tv shows, anime, manga, etc. If you spot any references, chances are that’s where they came from. I won’t list them, so you can have fun guessing! Let's just say I’ve stood on the shoulders of giants and did my best to honor my favorite works of art.

I wrote the music for this level, despite having little to no background in music. I wanted the level to have a completely unique feel - something that wouldn't be achieved by using someone elses track. I was worried that my music making skill level might not match the rest of what I created for this level, but thankfully, ambient music is more forgiving and works well as a background piece. I hope you enjoy it!

My friend gifted me a bass guitar, and although Im new to the instrument I was able to use it to record some of the droney parts of the track. The other sections were filled with sound effects created by myself, a friend, or used from free sound archive website, then edited with added reverb and stretched for more depth."

This map took countless hours definitely in the hundreds, though I didnt keep track. As I neared the end with the layout was fully complete and about 80% of the art done, Doug came in and helped me finish it. I want to give him full credit for everything he contributed, because honestly, this map probably wouldn’t have been completed without him. He was constantly encouraging me to finish the map and offering ideas. 

Map is bsp2 so a modern soure port with increased limits is needed.
