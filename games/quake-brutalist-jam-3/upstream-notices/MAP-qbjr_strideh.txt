QONQRETE: RESURFACED

Qonqrete was a map with a lot of problems. I cut it down massively during the first brutalist jam and what was left was a bit of a mess. Tight corridors and easily broken encounters. A QBJ3 remix let me take a stab at improving a few things, without massively rebuilding everything.

I started by deleting most of the encounters, all the weapons and starting over. I overhauled a lotta spaces to read a bit cleaner, optimised that absolute hell of out geometry and texture memory, added in a little more room to move and fixed up all the places I found monsters getting caught. 

Treating it essentially as a "hard mode" remix of the original, I made it much nastier and updated it with the latest Makkon textures and a darker, moodier atmosphere to suit. Enjoy!

- more enemies! (59/64/72 -> 86/98/115)
- more secrets! (2 -> 6)
- more efficient!
- a hidden little guy

Fix-up time: a week or two, give or take

Co-op is supported but not tested and will probably reveal some sneaky monster spawns.
